About us

It started with a yo-yo in 1998. A kid from Singapore who couldn't stop practising, won his first contest in 2000 and went on to represent Duncan Toys as a pro player.

In 2011 he launched Dement, a brand built by a player, for the people who play. Clothes that move with you, from the contest stage to the street.

Today Dement covers yo-yo’s and skill toys, clothing streetwear and gifts, plus DIY and craft, outdoor and adventure. Same spirit: limited drops, real design, no shortcuts.

Fajar Siddiq, Founder

How it started

2005 – 2008

Euphorya Clothing

by Euphorya

  • Euphorya was founded in 2005, starting with graphic design services at myspace.com and merchandise for local and international punk rock and hardcore bands in the LCHC, Lion City Hardcore and MCHC, Malaysia Hardcore Community scenes.
  • Supported the underground local scene in Singapore with DIY and silkscreen t-shirts for international bands Comeback Kid, and helped sell merchandise for Parkway Drive and 30+ bands, support local art & music scene for gigs and events
  • Made custom t-shirts, pin buttons and sticker designs, and later launched in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
2008 – 2011

Throw Clothing

by Euphorya

  • The label was rebranded from Euphorya Clothing to Throw Clothing in 2008
  • Launched in Jakarta and Bandung at the Indonesia Yo-Yo Contest Event
  • T-shirt silkscreen production was manufactured in Bandung, Indonesia
  • Sponsored the Malaysia Yo-Yo Championships 2008 for the top 3 winners
  • Released over 5 designs for the collection
  • Partnered with Indonesian investors and later launched in Singapore
  • One of its most iconic designs featured full-stretch printing and tie-dye
2012 – Present

Dement Clothing

by Euphorya

  • Became Dement Clothing by Euphorya in 2012, with the “Made in Yishun” spirit and the “Fight for Passion!” slogan.
  • Build community of hobby & skill toys for yo-yoing, kendama, running, fixie, skateboarding, gaming, streetwear modelling, action and extreme sports.
  • 2012 to 2016: 1,000+ units sold across 35 designs and collabs, about $21,225 in profit, reinvested to make more and give back to the community
  • Community projects include Throw+65 in Singapore and the Dement Yo-Yo Cup in Kuala Lumpur, 2014, with Kedaiyoyo.com
  • 2016-2026: Focusing on POD Print-On-Demand, B2B for corporate gifts, DIY & Craft, Outdoor & Adventures Community.
